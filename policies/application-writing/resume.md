# Résumé: selection and compression

The résumé is the same object every time: one locked template, one page by default, real text an applicant-tracking system can read. Employer names, job titles, dates, education, and certifications are printed by the system from the candidate's profile. You decide what goes under each role, the headline, the summary, and which skills to show. Selection is the craft: most of the work is choosing what to leave out.

## Headline

Three to ten words of positioning built from the candidate's own headline and proof, shaped toward this role. Examples of shape: "Operator who builds AI workflows for regulated teams", "Enterprise account executive · public sector". Never give the candidate a title they have not held. Cite the sources it rests on.

## Summary

Two or three sentences, under 60 words. First: what the candidate does and for whom, in plain words. Second: the single strongest result that matters for this role, with its number. Optional third: the capability this role needs most, proven by a source. No self-praise adjectives ("results-driven", "passionate", "dynamic", "seasoned").

## Bullets

- Start with a strong verb. Past tense for finished roles; present tense for the current role.
- Shape: action + what + how or for whom + result. When the number is strong, lead with it.
- One idea per bullet, usually 12 to 28 words. No first person. No filler ("responsible for", "helped with", "worked on", "various").
- Mirror the posting's concrete nouns (tools, customer types, domains, deliverables) when the source supports them. Do not mirror buzzwords.
- A bullet may only cite sources listed under its own role. A story belongs under its role; compress it to the action and the number.
- Do not repeat the same accomplishment in two bullets or open three bullets with the same verb.

## Selection

- Every role appears. The most relevant recent role gets four to six bullets; other recent roles two to four; older or unrelated roles one or two, or none if nothing helps this application.
- At most 20 bullets in total. Cut the weakest and least relevant first, even if impressive.
- Put each role's most relevant bullet first.

## Skills

Two to four groups, most relevant first, four to ten items each. Only skills that appear in the candidate's profile or sources. Drop unrelated ones. No proficiency ratings.

## Tailoring mode

- **REORDER_AND_TIGHTEN**: keep the candidate's wording. Select, reorder, trim, and swap in the posting's nouns only where the meaning is identical.
- **REWRITE_FROM_VERIFIED_FACTS**: rewrite freely for clarity and impact; every fact stays inside its sources.
- **AS_UPLOADED**: the candidate wants their résumé as written; return no bullets, headline, summary, or skills.
