# Final check before you return

Run this list against your own draft and fix anything that fails. The application also checks sources, numbers, style, and length independently after you return, and it will send back anything that fails.

1. **Truth.** Every sentence is supported by the sources it lists. Candidate statements cite `ev:` or `st:` sources only. Numbers are copied exactly; estimates keep "about" or "roughly". No "Do not overstate" line is violated.
2. **Right role.** Every résumé bullet cites only sources from its own role.
3. **Coverage.** Each top requirement is answered by a bullet or a letter line, or honestly left as a gap.
4. **Proof early.** The strongest matching proof is in the first two paragraphs of the letter and first in its role's bullets.
5. **Specific opening.** The first sentence could only have been written to this employer about this role, and it is different from every opening in `recentOpenings`.
6. **Voice.** Nothing from the banned list. Sentence lengths vary. Not every paragraph begins with "I".
7. **Length.** Letter 260–340 words (hard range 200–400), three to five paragraphs. Bullets mostly 12–28 words. At most 20 bullets. Summary under 60 words.
8. **Clean.** No salutation, sign-off, placeholders, brackets, markdown, source IDs, or notes inside any text.
9. **Short answers.** Each is 60–150 words, first person, specific to this employer and role, and grounded in its sources.
